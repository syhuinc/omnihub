Delete your Omni Hub account and data

Omni Hub never requires an account — everything works fully on your device without one. This page is only relevant if you chose to sign in with Google (Profile ▸ Cloud Sync) to sync your data across devices, and now want that account and its synced data permanently removed.

How to delete

1

From the app (fastest)

Open Omni Hub, go to Profile, and under Cloud Sync tap Delete My Account. Confirm the prompt. This deletes everything immediately — no waiting period.

2

Without the app installed

If you no longer have Omni Hub installed, email us from the same address your Google account uses to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Omni Hub account":

syhu.inc@gmail.com

We'll delete your account and all synced data within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account — your Firebase Authentication identity (name, email, profile photo) is deleted entirely.
  • Everything synced to it — Notes, Checklist, Expense Tracker, Budget, Subscription Calculator, Debt Tracker, Alarms, and your Vault's encrypted ciphertext, all removed from our servers.
  • Local data on the device you deleted from — cleared automatically as part of the in-app deletion. (If you have Omni Hub installed on other devices, their local copies aren't reachable remotely — uninstall the app there too, or open it once signed out to clear it.)
This can't be undone. There's no recovery period or backup retention on our end — once deleted, the data is gone.
